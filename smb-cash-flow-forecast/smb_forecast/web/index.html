Read the model correctly

Break-even estimates operating revenue needed to cover fixed costs and payroll. It excludes interest, taxes, capital spending, and cash timing.

Runway uses average cash burn over the last three available months, before new debt draws. It includes capital spending and principal payments. “No recent burn” does not guarantee future cash availability.

Taxes use your assumed rate on positive operating income less interest. Tax rules, loss carryforwards, depreciation, inventory, sales taxes, and distributions are outside this model.

This tool provides calculations, not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice.
